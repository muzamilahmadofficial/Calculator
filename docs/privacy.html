Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

Summary: MA Calculator does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or sensitive device information. All mathematical calculations and calculation histories remain strictly on your local device.

1. Information Collection and Use

MA Calculator is designed with a strict privacy-first architecture:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or device identifiers.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or link any external accounts to use any feature of the application.
  • Local SQLite Storage: Your calculation history is saved solely in your device's local sandboxed database (Room/SQLite). It is never synced to external cloud servers.
  • Voice Input: When you use voice input for dictating calculations, the speech recognition is handled either on-device or via your operating system's standard speech-to-text service (Android Recognizer). No audio recordings are collected or stored by MA Calculator.

2. Permissions Requested

MA Calculator requires minimal permissions to operate:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used exclusively to provide subtle tactile haptic feedback when tapping keypad buttons. Can be disabled in device settings at any time.

The application requests zero dangerous permissions and does not request access to your internet connection, camera, microphone, contacts, files, or location.

3. Third-Party Services & Tracking

MA Calculator does not integrate:

  • Third-party analytics SDKs (e.g. Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Mixpanel).
  • Third-party advertising networks (e.g. AdMob, Unity Ads).
  • Cross-app tracking tools or trackers.

4. Children’s Privacy

MA Calculator does not address anyone under the age of 13 specifically, nor do we knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. The application is completely safe for users of all ages.

5. Data Security

Because all data remains on your local hardware device, security is governed by your Android device's built-in operating system security and sandbox protection. We recommend keeping your device updated with the latest security patches.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via your Google Play Console developer contact email.
